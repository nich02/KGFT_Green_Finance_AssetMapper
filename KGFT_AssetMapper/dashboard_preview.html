How to read the results

Green identified means a strong preliminary keyword match. Manual assessment means a partial or ambiguous match. Not green includes records without a configured positive match or with a potential exclusion flag.

A keyword result is a prioritisation aid, not a KGFT certification decision.

About this project

KGFT-informed screening identifies candidate green activities from loan purposes and product descriptions. The workflow is exclusion-first, explainable and designed for human validation. The screening categories are preliminary.
